## Scope

Public site home (Persuade), department page (Read), dashboard shell (Operate). Phase 0 of the NCIT build.

## Audience and job

Prospective students and parents on phones decide on a programme and apply; current students check notices and events; content owners (Super Admin, HODs, Event and ECA admins) maintain their own areas in the dashboard.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The classic university site played straight at Harvard/MIT craft level: photography-led banner, clear mega-menus, editorial news, events and notices, leadership messages, a dedicated apply band. It refuses gimmicks; NCIT's royal blue and Om red carry the identity.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool canvas (#f6f7fb) grounds, royal blue #182c85 as the institutional colour (utility bar, footer, apply band), Om red #cf1f29 only for Apply actions and urgent states, sky blue for accents on dark. Source Serif 4 display (optical sizes), Mukta for text and UI (Devanagari-ready). Square-ish 3px corners, 1px rules, divided lists rather than cards.

STORY: A visitor learns what NCIT teaches and where (first viewport), finds a department and its semester courses (two taps), sees current news, events and notices, hears from leadership, and applies from any page via the persistent red Apply button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Preview strip, navy utility bar with audience links and Sign in, white sticky header with emblem, wordmark, six-item nav with Academics/Admissions mega-menus, search, red Apply now. Full-bleed banner carousel (Super Admin managed) with serif display headline bottom-left over a photo, two actions, progress-bar slide controls with pause; quick-links row directly under it.

FORM: Category canon chosen by the client (standing exit); grounded list position: not applicable. seed:4f9772d400482755

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
