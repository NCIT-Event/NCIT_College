Technology education, close to the people who use it.

Nepal College of Information Technology teaches engineering, information technology and management in Balkumari, Lalitpur. Programmes follow the Pokhara University curriculum, and students spend much of their time in labs, studios and project teams.

Every department, club and event on this site is kept up to date by the people who run it, so what you read here is what is happening on campus.
