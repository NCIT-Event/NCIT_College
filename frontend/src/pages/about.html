An IT and engineering college in Lalitpur

Nepal College of Information Technology (NCIT) teaches engineering, information technology and management programmes affiliated to Pokhara University, on its campus in Balkumari, Lalitpur. The college was established in 2001.

Every department, club and event on this website is kept up to date by the people who run it: Heads of Department edit their own department pages, and the events, clubs, admissions and careers offices publish their own news.
