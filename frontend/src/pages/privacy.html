Privacy notice

What this website collects, who sees it and how long it is kept.

Preview. The content service isn't running, so this page shows preview data.

Draft for the college to review. This notice describes what the website actually does. The college should confirm the retention periods and the contact person before launch.

What we collect, and why

  • Admission applications: your name, date of birth, contact details, guardian, previous study and the documents you upload, to assess your application and contact you about it.
  • Job applications: your name, contact details, qualifications, cover letter and CV, to consider you for the vacancy.
  • Alumni registration: your name, programme, year, Pokhara University registration number and, if you choose, your job, city, LinkedIn and photo, to verify you and (only if you agree) list you in the alumni directory.
  • Event registrations and club membership: your college account name and email, so organisers know who's coming.
  • Contact form: your name, email, phone (optional) and message, to reply to you.
  • Signing in: your email address, and a record of when you signed in, for security.

Who can see it

Only the office responsible: the admissions office sees applications, HR sees job applications, the alumni office sees alumni registrations, event and club organisers see their own lists. Uploaded documents and CVs are stored privately and are never public web addresses. The alumni directory never shows email addresses, phone numbers or registration numbers.

Cookies

The website uses only the cookies it needs to keep you signed in and to protect forms from misuse. There is no advertising or tracking, and no analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

Admission and job applications: for the admission cycle or recruitment and the period the college's records policy requires afterwards To be confirmed. Alumni profiles: until you ask us to remove them. Email and access logs: for security reviews, then deleted To be confirmed.

Your choices

You can see and correct your own application, job application or alumni profile after signing in. To ask for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted, email info@ncit.edu.np.
