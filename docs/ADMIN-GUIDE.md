# Dashboard guide for NCIT staff

A short, practical guide for each role, to use in the handover training sessions. Everyone signs in at **ncit.edu.np/login.html** with their college Google account (`@ncit.edu.np`); there are no separate passwords. Your menu shows only what your role allows.

Good habits for everyone:
- **Times are Nepal time** everywhere in the dashboard.
- **Changes reach the website within about 5 seconds.**
- **The bell** (top of every page and the dashboard) shows notifications for you and your role: new applications, join requests, decisions about you. Click one to open it; **Mark all as read** clears the badge.
- **Text is plain text:** leave a blank line between paragraphs. Formatting codes are shown as typed.
- **Photos** are resized automatically. Fill in the photo description: screen-reader users rely on it.
- **"Published"** decides whether something is on the website. Untick it to hide something without deleting it.
- **Everything you change is recorded** in the Audit log, with your name.
- Stuck? Ask a Super Admin; they can see what happened in the Audit log.

---

## Super Admin

You manage **who can do what**, and can open every area.

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Give someone a role (HOD of a department, Event Admin …) | Users & access → **Grant a role** → their college email → role. They get it next time they sign in; it can have an expiry date |
| Take a role away / someone leaves | Users & access → their row → remove the role, or **Suspend** the account. Takes effect on their next click |
| Many people at once | Users & access → **Import CSV** (email, role, department) |
| See what happened | **Audit log**: filter by action, search by name |
| Emails people should have received | **Email log**: *Failed* entries can be sent again |
| Applicants and alumni accounts | Users & access → account type **Applicants & alumni** (suspend if misused) |

Safety rules built in: there is always at least one Super Admin, and nobody can suspend themselves.

**Training exercise:** grant a colleague *Event Admin* with an expiry date next week, check it in the Audit log, then remove it.

## Content Admin (website content)

| Task | Where | Tips |
|---|---|---|
| Home page banner or an ad | **Banners & ads** | Set *Show from / Show until* to schedule it; it appears and disappears by itself. Use landscape photos, at least 2000 px wide |
| News article | **News** | *Publish at* in the future schedules it. Each article gets its own page |
| Notice (exam routine, holiday, result) | **Notices** | Attach the PDF, or a link for results on another site. **Pin** keeps it at the top; *Remove after* takes it down automatically |
| Leadership messages | **Leadership messages** | One each for the Executive Director, Director and Principal. Edit the existing one |
| Facilities | **Facilities** | Arrows set the order |

**Training exercise:** schedule a banner for tomorrow (it shows as *Scheduled*), publish a notice with a PDF and pin it, then check both on the website.

## Head of Department (your department page)

Open **My department**. You can edit only your own department.

| Section | What to keep current |
|---|---|
| Overview | Introduction, contact details, your message and photo |
| Programmes | Each programme, and its courses semester by semester (code, title, credits) |
| Faculty | Name, designation, specialisation, email, photo. They also appear in the **Faculty directory** and in site search |
| Labs | Name, description, photo |
| Testimonials | Student quotes; tick *Approved* to show one |

Use the arrows to set the order. Untick *Published* to hide the whole page while you work on it.

**Training exercise:** add a faculty member with a photo, reorder the list, then find them in the Faculty directory and in search.

## Event Admin

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Add an event | **Events** → Add event: name, category, start and end, venue, organiser, cover photo, optional programme PDF |
| Take registrations | Tick **Take registrations**, set **Places** (0 = no limit) and, if needed, when registration closes. When it's full, new sign-ups join a **waiting list** and move up automatically when someone cancels |
| See who's coming | **Registrations** on the event: mark **Attended** on the day, **Admit** someone from the waiting list, **Download CSV** |

**Training exercise:** create an event with 2 places, register with three accounts, and watch the third move up when one cancels.

## ECA Admin (clubs)

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Club pages | **Clubs**: description, meeting time, staff coordinator, photo, logo; *Accepting new members* switches the "Ask to join" button |
| Approve students | **Join requests** (the menu shows how many are waiting): **Approve** or **Reject** |
| Committee | Club → **Members** → type a position (President, Secretary …) and Save. People with a position are shown on the club page |
| Club news | **Club activities**: what the club did, with a date and photo |

## Admission Officer

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Open an intake | **Intakes**: name (e.g. *Fall 2026*), when applications open and close |
| Programmes on offer | **Programmes on offer**: programme, seats, who can apply, fee note |
| Review applications | **Applications**: filter by status or intake, open one, look at the documents, choose the **next step** |

The next steps:

| Step | Use it when | The applicant gets |
|---|---|---|
| Under review | You start looking at it | an email |
| Ask for more documents | Something is missing (say exactly what) | an email; they can edit and send again |
| Accept | Offering a place (add fee and enrolment dates in the message) | an email |
| Waiting list / Don't accept | | an email |

- **Internal note:** for the office only, never shown to the applicant.
- **Email them:** untick to correct a status quietly.
- **Download CSV:** exports the current filter.

**Training exercise:** apply yourself from `/apply` with a personal email, then ask for more documents, see it from the applicant's side, and accept.

## HR Officer

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Advertise a job | **Vacancies**: title, faculty or staff, positions, closing time, the role and requirements (one per line), optional PDF |
| Review applicants | **Applicants** on a vacancy, or **Job applications**: open the CV, then **Shortlist**, **Invite to interview** (the date, time and place are required and are emailed), **Make an offer** or **Don't take forward** |

## Alumni Coordinator

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Verify registrations | **Alumni verification** → *Waiting* → check name, programme, year and **Pokhara University registration number** against the records → **Verify**, or **Can't verify** with the reason (they're emailed it and can correct it) |
| Home page alumni | On a verified profile that agreed to be listed, tick **Feature on the home page**. The four most recently verified featured alumni are shown |

Only verified alumni who agreed to be listed appear in the directory, and never with their email, phone or registration number.

## Every member of staff and every student

**My activity** lists the events you registered for and the clubs you belong to. Register for events and join clubs from the **Events** and **ECA & clubs** pages on the website.
